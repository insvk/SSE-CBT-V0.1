Computer Based Assessment Examination Guidelines • Candidate Instructions

1. General Information & Authoritative Server Clock

  • Total duration of examination is 180 minutes (3 hours).
  • The clock is synchronized with the central examination server. The timer in the top-right corner displays the remaining time available. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will automatically submit.
  • Each correct answer awards +4.0 marks. Each incorrect response incurs -1.0 mark. Unanswered questions receive 0 marks.

2. Question Palette Status Symbols

01 Not Visited: You have not visited the question yet.
02 Not Answered: You have visited the question but have not answered it yet.
03 Answered: You have answered the question.
04 Marked for Review: You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
05 Answered & Marked for Review: The question will be evaluated in final scoring.
